Teamwork · once per battle

Once per battle, choose a healthy bench companion and preview its help before confirming. Refresh heals the lead and guards; Cover makes a weaker strike and guards; Rally makes a stronger strike. Each uses your full turn, and the opponent replies if it is still standing.

EXPERIMENTAL ONLY · OPTIONAL AUDIO

Original exploration and battle loops.

Experimental includes two original loops alongside its short effects. Game audio is off by default; the Sound toggle enables it. These previews are manual controls with no autoplay. Explore, battle, capture, and build a team.

EXPERIMENTAL ONLY · Release v4 · browser-ready

Beyond the Acropolis.

The new expedition continues east through modern towns, cultivated roads, old waterworks, coastal ruins, and the Summit Arena. Each challenge keeps a return path open.

Acropolis → Olive Town + Aqueduct → Quarry Town + Quarry → Salt Coast + Cistern → Windmill Town + Wind Temple → Summit Trail + Observatory → Summit Arena

Six restoration puzzles and badgesRestore sluices, quarry lifts, echo channels, wind vanes, lenses, and summit beacons. Wrong steps reset only the mechanism.
Thirty six campaign battlesBuild a six-member team through varied local battles, meet Niko six times, then face six new leaders. Combined with the base game, all 46 are cleared in the verified campaign.
Nearby recoveryCave Medic, Coast Rest Stop, towns, and mountain camps restore the team. During the expedition, defeat returns you to your recorded recovery checkpoint. The Journal offers travel to checkpoints already visited.
