ATLANTIS + OLYMPUS · EXPERIMENTAL ONLY

Two new destinations define the v7 expansion.

Atlantis is an underwater route reached from the harbor with free scuba gear. Olympus branches from Summit Trail and leads through an inhabited mountain, a lava-heated forge, trials, and the summit.

AtlantisSeven connected areas: reef, city, Siren House, archive, caves, grotto, and Poseidon Temple.
OlympusSix connected areas: trail, hermit hut, cave, cloud pass, gate, and summit.
Travel stays reversibleHarbor and lower mountain routes remain open, with healing checkpoints and safe retreats.
